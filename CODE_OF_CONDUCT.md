# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in AboueiChecker a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes using welcoming language, respecting viewpoints,
giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is
best for the project. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or
imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing other people's private
information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported through GitHub's private
abuse and issue reporting on this repository:

<https://github.com/Erfan-Abouei/Abouei_Checker/issues>

Do not include secrets, credentials, or private personal information in any
report. The maintainer will review reports, investigate, and take appropriate
action, which may include warning, temporary, or permanent removal from project
spaces. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent GitHub's tooling
allows.

This is a single-maintainer project; there is no separate moderation team.
If GitHub's reporting tooling is unavailable to you, open a minimal public
issue asking for a private contact channel without describing the incident
in detail.
